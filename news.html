MATCH

Knights stun Warriors in Eden Park semi-final

Newcastle held on for a 12-10 win over the Warriors at Eden Park on 20 September.

The match was level at 6-6 at half-time before Newcastle moved ahead in the second half. Halasima scored again late in the game as the Warriors pushed for a comeback.

Source: NRL.com
TEAM

DWZ and Halasima return for the big clash

Dallin Watene-Zelezniak and Leka Halasima were confirmed in the starting side.

Watene-Zelezniak was returning from a broken collarbone and was set to make his 250th NRL appearance, while Halasima came into the centres.

Source: Warriors.kiwi
FANS

Auckland lights up for Wahs Weekend

Major Auckland landmarks were lit in Warriors colours ahead of the sold-out Eden Park final.

The Warriors said the expected crowd was more than 48,000, with fans also able to use public transport to get to Eden Park with their match tickets.

Source: Warriors.kiwi
TEAM

James Fisher-Harris wins Simon Mannering Medal

The co-captain was named the Warriors' premier player of the 2026 season.

Fisher-Harris received the award at the club's 2026 awards night in Auckland.

Source: Warriors.kiwi
TEAM

Senior players return for the final regular-season round

Te Maire Martin, Chanel Harris-Tavita and Charnze Nicoll-Klokstad returned for the Sea Eagles clash.

The Warriors went into that round on top of the table and were preparing for their finals campaign.

Source: Warriors.kiwi
TEAM

Eddie Ieremia-Toeava named NRL Rookie of the Year

The young forward was recognised at the Warriors' 2026 awards night.

The awards also recognised Jacob Laban as the NRL Players' Player of the Year and Erin Clark as the NRL People's Choice winner.

Source: Warriors.kiwi
